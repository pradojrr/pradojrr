# Ronaldo de Oliveira Prado Junior

Electrical Engineering undergraduate student at **Universidade Federal de Mato Grosso do Sul (UFMS)**.  
Founder, Captain, and Powertrain Director of **F.R.O.G. Racing**, the first Formula SAE Electric team in the state of Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil.

Focused on high-performance powertrain engineering, power electronics, Model-Based Design (MBD), and vehicle telemetry analytics targeting Formula 1 industrial placements.

---

### Core Engineering Disciplines

- **Electric Powertrain & High Voltage:** 380V accumulator architecture, packaging, and Battery Management System (BMS) integration complying with Formula SAE safety rules.
- **Electric Motor Drives & Control:** Field-Oriented Control (FOC), Space Vector PWM (SVPWM), and decoupled d-q current regulation for Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors (PMSM).
- **Simulation & Modeling:** Model-Based Design in MATLAB/Simulink, numerical ODE solvers in Python for longitudinal vehicle dynamics.
- **Embedded Systems & Telemetry:** CAN bus data acquisition, vehicle diagnostics, and automated data pipelines using the FastF1 API and Streamlit.

---

### Key Technical Projects

#### [High-Voltage PMSM FOC Drive Architecture](https://github.com/pradojrr/fsae-pmsm-foc-drive)
Model-Based Design and closed-loop control simulation of an 86 kW PMSM traction drive. Implements inner-loop PI current tracking, inverter switching modeling, and SVPWM modulation for a 380V DC-link bus.
- **Stack:** MATLAB, Simulink, Control System Toolbox.

#### [F1 Longitudinal Dynamics & Telemetry Analytics Suite]([https://github.com/pradojrr/f1-telemetry-dynamics-suite](https://github.com/pradojrr/f1-vehicle-dynamics-ers-sim)
Vehicle dynamics analytics platform combining real FIA telemetry with a numerical ODE solver for longitudinal acceleration, aerodynamic drag, rolling resistance, and hybrid ERS State of Charge (SoC).
- **Stack:** Python, FastF1, SciPy, Pandas, Streamlit, Plotly.
